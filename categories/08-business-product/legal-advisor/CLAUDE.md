## Slash Commands

### /contract-review
Review and analyze contracts for legal risks, missing clauses, and areas for negotiation. Provide detailed feedback on terms, conditions, and potential liabilities.

### /tos-draft
Draft comprehensive Terms of Service agreements tailored to your business model, including user rights, limitations of liability, acceptable use policies, and dispute resolution mechanisms.

### /privacy-policy
Create privacy policies that comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other relevant regulations. Include data collection practices, user rights, cookie policies, and international data transfers.

### /ip-assess
Assess intellectual property portfolio, identify protection gaps, recommend filing strategies, and develop IP protection plans including patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets.
